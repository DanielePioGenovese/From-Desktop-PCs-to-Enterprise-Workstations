# Report a mistake

The book has been proofread and fact-checked, but across roughly 130,000 words of
technical material — hundreds of acronyms, price tables and model numbers — something
always slips through. Reports are welcome, however small.

**Check the [errata](ERRATA.md) first:** the mistake may already be known and queued
for the next revision.

Reports in English or Italian are both fine.

## How to report

[Open a new report](https://github.com/DanielePioGenovese/From-Desktop-PCs-to-Enterprise-Workstations/issues/new/choose)
and pick one of the three templates. A GitHub account is required; it is free.

| Template | When to use it |
|---|---|
| **Typo or formatting error** | Spelling, grammar, punctuation, formatting, misaligned tables, broken links |
| **Technical error** | Wrong data: a specification, a price, a compatibility claim, a technically incorrect statement |
| **Suggestion** | An unclear explanation, a missing topic, a proposed improvement |

## What makes a report useful

The same three things in every case:

1. **Where**: language (English or Italian), chapter and section. If you are reading the
   PDF, the page number is perfectly fine.
2. **What it currently says**: paste the exact sentence. A few words are enough to find it.
3. **What it should say**, and why.

For technical errors, **a source makes the difference**: the manufacturer's specification
page, official documentation, a review from a serious outlet. Not because I doubt you, but
because prices and specifications move fast and I need to be able to verify before
correcting.

A report without a source is still useful — I will verify it myself.

## No pull request needed

The book is released under [CC BY-NC-ND 4.0](LICENSE), which does not permit distributing
modified versions. The Markdown files in `ITA/` and `ENG/` are generated from the original
manuscript, so an edit made here would be overwritten at the next revision anyway.

**Open an issue instead: it is the right route and it is faster.** Corrections go into the
manuscript and from there into every format — PDF, website and Markdown — at the following
release.

## Timing

This is a personal project, maintained in spare time. I read everything, but replies may
take a few days. Corrections are collected and published in batches with a new release,
not one at a time.

Anyone who reports a confirmed technical error is credited in the [errata](ERRATA.md),
unless they would rather not — just say so in the issue.

## Questions about the content

If you have not found a mistake but want to discuss something in the book — advice on a
build, a doubt about a component choice — use the **Suggestion** template, or reach me on
LinkedIn. Issues stay public, so the answer is useful to whoever comes next.

Thank you: every report improves the next edition.
